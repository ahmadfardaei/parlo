# Grammar PDFs vs Parlo lessons

Source: 14 PDFs in `Italian Roadmap/Grammars`, used as reference only. They are business-oriented, written for one learner who already speaks English and French. New lessons are in `grammar-6.json`.

## 01 Congiuntivo
- Says the congiuntivo is a matter of perspective (subjective reading vs fact), not a list of triggers.
- Four areas: opinion, doubt/possibility, wishes/requests, counterfactual se-clauses.
- Choosing the tense: presente / passato / imperfetto / trapassato by the main verb's tense and time.
- Traps: "I think that" + indicative, French *espérer* + indicative, same subject means infinitive.
- **Covered by:** cong, g-cong-imp, g-cong-passato, g-ipotetico.
- **Gaps:** (1) No single table of the *concordanza dei tempi* that shows all four congiuntivo tenses together. Each lesson shows only its own part. (2) The congiuntivo trapassato outside se-clauses (*pensavo che avesse già ricevuto*) only gets a passing note. (3) *chiedere / esigere che* + congiuntivo is missing. (4) Nothing says that spoken Italian often uses the indicative after *penso che*, or that *non so se* + indicative is standard.
- **PDF slips:** "Speriamo che riusciamo" is marked correct. "Vi ringraziamo affinché possiate…" is not idiomatic. The *penso che siamo* paragraph is muddled.

## 02 Passato (pp / imperfetto / trapassato)
- Narrator's frame: the imperfetto is the background, the passato prossimo moves the action, the trapassato is "before the before".
- Three typical setups: pure description, a sequence of events, or both mixed.
- Traps: telling a story only in the passato prossimo, *è stato arrabbiato* vs *era arrabbiato*, *ho andato*, skipping the trapassato.
- **Covered by:** pp, imp, ppimp, g-trapassato (well covered).
- **Gaps:** (1) *infinito passato* (*dopo aver bevuto*, *dopo essere arrivati*) is not taught anywhere. (2) A whole finished period judged with the passato prossimo (*è stata una brutta esperienza*) vs the imperfetto for how it felt at the time: this could go in ppimp as one more "use".

## 03 Cortesia (functional)
- Use the condizionale instead of the presente for any request (vorrei, potrei, sarebbe possibile, mi servirebbe).
- Fixed formulas: *Le sarei grato se potesse…*, *Le chiederei cortesemente…*. Turning statements into questions. Impersonal phrasing (*c'è stato un errore*). Cushion words.
- Refusals in three parts: regret + reason + alternative. Bad news. Disagreement. Proposals to someone senior.
- Rule of thumb: at most two politeness devices per request.
- **Covered by:** nothing as a function. The cond lesson covers *vorrei/potrebbe* only as forms.
- **New lesson:** g-cortesia (B1).

## 04 Pronomi
- Direct, indirect, ci, ne, combined pronouns. Participle agreement with lo/la/li/le and with ne.
- Verbs that take an indirect object in Italian (telefonare, rispondere). Pronoun position with infinitive, imperative and modals.
- Set phrases: ci sto, ci provo, ce la faccio, ci sentiamo.
- **Covered by:** g-dobj, g-iobj, g-ci-ne, g-combined (well covered).
- **Gaps:** (1) Pronominal verbs (*farcela, andarsene, cavarsela, prendersela*) are missing; *ce la faccio* is never explained. (2) Formal *loro* (*scrivo loro*) is only a table cell. (3) Repeating a fronted *di*-phrase with ne (*Del budget ne abbiamo parlato*) is colloquial and is not explained.
- **PDF slips:** "I dati? Me li serve" (should be *Mi servono*). "A Marta le ho parlato del problema" is given as the correction.

## 05 Negoziazione (functional)
- Disagreeing in three moves: acknowledge, concede, then contrast constructively with an alternative.
- Concession: anche se + indicative. benché / sebbene / per quanto + congiuntivo. nonostante / malgrado + noun or congiuntivo. pur + gerund.
- Contrast: ma / però / tuttavia, invece, d'altra parte, per contro, in compenso, anzi.
- Formulas: *mi permetto di…*, *mi verrebbe da…*, *non sarei così sicuro che…*, *non è del tutto…*, *ho l'impressione che…*.
- **Covered by:** g-connectors (anche se / benché basics only).
- **New lesson:** g-negoziazione (B2).

## 06 Connettivi
- Six families: causal, consecutive, additive, adversative, explanatory, temporal/sequencing.
- Functional fillers: *detto questo, in effetti, in realtà, sostanzialmente, vale la pena*.
- Openers, transitions and closers for paragraphs (*passiamo ora a…, tornando a…, alla luce di quanto detto…*).
- **Covered by:** g-connectors (cause, result, contrast, concession, cioè), plus g-causa now.
- **Gaps:** (1) Additive connectors (*inoltre, peraltro, oltre a, non solo… ma anche*) are missing. (2) Explanatory ones (*ovvero, vale a dire, in particolare, ad esempio*) are missing. (3) Sequencing (*innanzitutto, in primo luogo, infine, nel frattempo, contemporaneamente*) is missing. (4) Discourse markers (*detto questo, in effetti, sostanzialmente*) and topic transitions are missing. Together these could make one B2 lesson, "Organizzare il discorso". I didn't write it because it overlaps with g-connectors.

## 07 Futuro e ipotesi
- Futuro semplice, including the futuro of probability. **Futuro anteriore** (finished before a future point, and probability in the past).
- Condizionale semplice and passato, future in the past, and the three types of periodo ipotetico, including the mixed type.
- **Covered by:** fut, cond, g-cond-passato, g-ipotetico, g-discourse.
- **Gap filled:** the futuro anteriore had no lesson → **new lesson g-fut-anteriore (B1)**.
- **PDF slips:** it presents *quando arrivo, ti chiamerò* as wrong. It is normal in speech; the fut lesson already handles this correctly.

## 08 Incertezza (functional)
- A scale of certainty. Modals (*dovrebbe*, *deve aver…*). *Può darsi che* + congiuntivo.
- Adverbs: sicuramente / probabilmente / forse / magari / chissà. Opinion verbs + congiuntivo.
- Impersonal hearsay: *pare che, sembrerebbe che, a quanto pare*. The journalistic condizionale.
- Calibrating commitments: *dovrebbe finire a marzo, salvo imprevisti*.
- **Covered by:** pieces only (fut probability use, cond reported news, cong doubt).
- **New lesson:** g-incertezza (B1).

## 09 Causa e conseguenza (functional)
- Cause: perché / poiché / dato che / siccome / visto che + indicative. a causa di / grazie a + noun. in quanto. Causal gerund (same subject). *il fatto che* + congiuntivo.
- Consequence: quindi / dunque / pertanto / perciò / di conseguenza. così… che, tanto… da, al punto che.
- Purpose: in modo che + congiuntivo / in modo da + infinitive, affinché + congiuntivo.
- **Covered by:** g-connectors (perché, quindi, siccome note).
- **New lesson:** g-causa (B1).
- **PDF slip:** "Il problema è al punto che dobbiamo…" is unidiomatic.

## 10 Istruzioni (functional)
- Four forms: tu/voi imperative, Lei imperative, infinitive for written instructions, *si* for describing procedures.
- Negative tu = non + infinitive. Pronoun position. Softeners: *potresti, sarebbe da, ti andrebbe di, quando puoi, ti dispiacerebbe*.
- **Covered by:** g-imperative (forms), g-si, g-passive (*va fatto*).
- **New lesson:** g-istruzioni (B1), focused on choosing the register rather than on the forms.

## 11 Impersonale
- si impersonale vs si passivante. The past with essere and agreement. *uno* + verb (colloquial). Passive with essere/venire. Passive infinitive.
- Warning against chains of English-style passives.
- **Covered by:** g-si, g-passive (well covered).
- **Gaps:** (1) The colloquial *uno* (*uno pensa che…*) is missing. (2) Advice on style (alternating active, si and passive so prose doesn't get heavy) is missing. This now appears briefly in g-registro.
- **PDF slip:** "Il report è venuto generato" is called "technically possible". It is wrong; g-passive is right.

## 12 Preposizioni
- Tendencies of di / a / da / in / su / per / con / tra. Verb + preposition collocations by area (communication, thinking, action, feelings, dependence).
- pensare a / di / che. credere a / in. Verbs with a direct object (ascoltare, aspettare, cercare, guardare).
- **Covered by:** g-prep, g-prep-art, g-prep-place-time. Only 4 infinitive patterns are covered, plus a few verbs in g-ci-ne / g-dobj.
- **Gap filled:** a systematic verb + preposition lesson → **new lesson g-verbi-prep (B1)**.

## 13 Genere e numero
- Masculine nouns in -a (il problema, il pianeta, il poeta). Feminine nouns in -o (la mano, la foto). Masculine plurals that become feminine (le braccia, le uova).
- Nouns in -e: learn them with their article. Nouns invariable in the plural (including those in -i: la crisi, l'analisi, la tesi).
- Gender changes meaning: il capitale / la capitale, il fine / la fine, il fronte / la fronte.
- **Covered by:** g-nouns, g-articles.
- **Gaps:** (1) Invariable nouns in **-i** (crisi, analisi, tesi, sintesi) are missing from the "no change" table. (2) Nouns whose meaning depends on gender are missing. (3) Double plurals with different meanings (i bracci / le braccia, i muri / le mura) are missing. (4) *il pianeta, il poeta, il pilota* (masculine in -ta) are not covered. These are small enough to add as notes to g-nouns.
- **PDF slips:** "biro" is said to come from "biroide" (it comes from László Bíró). The fix offered for "la chiave del successo sono i dati" is odd.

## 14 Registro scritto (functional)
- Getting from "correct" to "professional": use a participle instead of *che* + verb, precise verbs instead of fare/dire/avere, and institutional vocabulary.
- Strategic impersonal forms. Opening and closing formulas. Structured long sentences. Punctuation (; : « »).
- Avoid baroque overkill and literal translations from English.
- **Covered by:** nothing (g-subj covers tu/Lei only).
- **New lesson:** g-registro (B2).
