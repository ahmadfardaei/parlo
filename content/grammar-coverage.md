# Grammar coverage audit: A1 → B2

Scope: the 52 lessons in `src/data/grammar.json`, read in full (meaning, uses, form tables, notes, irregulars, mistakes; exercises searched by keyword).
Reference: the A1–B2 grammar inventories of the *Profilo della lingua italiana* (Council of Europe level descriptors) and the CILS / CELI syllabi up to B2 (CILS Due, CELI 3), from knowledge.
Levels below are the level at which a point is normally introduced. A point counts as **Covered** only if it is taught (explained with a rule or a use), not if it merely appears in an example.

Legend: **Covered** (lesson id) · **Partly** (what's missing) · **Missing**

### Overall verdict

The tense/mood system, pronouns, prepositions and the B2 functional/discourse layer are well covered and go beyond most course books (pronominal verbs, venire/andare passive, mixed hypotheticals, journalistic condizionale). The holes are concentrated in three places:

- **A2 "small words"** that have no home: adverbs, indefinites, ordinals, stressed pronouns.
- **Non-finite verb forms as clause builders** (gerund, participle, causative fare/lasciare + infinitive). These are core B2 exam material (CILS Due / CELI 3 reading and the "trasformazione" tasks).
- **The subjunctive as a system** rather than lesson by lesson: concordanza dei tempi and the full set of subordinating conjunctions and relative-clause uses.

Seven new lessons (items 1–7) plus additions to existing lessons would close every B2 gap without splitting topics into micro-lessons.
