Privacy

i understand, but… · last updated 31 October 2026

In short: without an account, your progress stays on your device. With an account, we keep your progress to sync it between your devices. We do not sell data. There are no advertisements, no tracking and no analytics.

Who is responsible

This app is run by Ahmad Fardaei, a private person. Contact: ahmad.fardaei@gmail.com.

Without an account

Your progress, your saved and hidden words, and your settings are stored only in your browser on your device. We do not receive them.

With an account

If you create an account (with email or with Google), we store:

  • your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name from Google;
  • the name you choose in your profile and your study settings;
  • your learning progress: what you reviewed and when, what you saved or hid, your scores and your texts.

We use this data only to keep your progress in sync on your devices. The legal basis is the service you ask for (GDPR, Article 6(1)(b)).

Other companies we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and progress.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app, the audio and the PDFs. Like every web server, it receives your IP address when your browser asks for a file.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google", only if you choose it.

The fonts come from our own site, not from Google.

Feedback

If you send feedback, it is an email to the address above. It includes the page you were on, your level and your browser, so we can understand the problem. We use it only to improve the app.

Your rights

  • Download a copy of your progress at any time: Profile → Account.
  • Delete your account at any time: Profile → Account. This erases your account and all your progress.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your data: write to the address above.
  • If you think we use your data wrongly, you can complain to your data protection authority.
