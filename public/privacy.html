Privacy

i understand, but… · last updated 28 October 2026

i understand, but… is a free, non-commercial app for learning Italian. This page explains what it stores about you and why.

Without an account

Your progress, starred deck and settings are stored only in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

With an account

If you create an account (with email or with Google), we store:

  • your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name as provided by Google;
  • the name you choose in your profile, and your study settings;
  • your learning progress: which words, verbs and phrases you've reviewed, when they're next due, which you've starred, and your grammar exercise scores.

This is used only to sync your progress across your devices. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything else. There are no ads and no tracking or analytics.

Where it's stored

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database and login provider. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Google sign-in is provided by Google; the app only receives your basic profile (name and email).

Your choices

  • Download a backup of your progress at any time from the Account page.
  • Delete your account from the Account page. This permanently erases your account and all your progress.
  • Questions: contact the app's owner via github.com/ahmadfardaei/parlo.
