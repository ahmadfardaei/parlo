Why it is free

Many people learn a language because they must. They move to a new country, they start a new job, or they have a family in a new language.

A good course should not depend on money. So this app is free:

  • no subscription and no paid level;
  • no advertisements;
  • no account needed. Your progress stays on your device.

You can sign in only to keep your progress on more than one device.
